# Target: senorita
Common name: Señorita wrasse
Scientific name used in this project: Oxyjulis californica
YOLO class: 0 (senorita)

Distinguishing features: slender, elongated body; small pointed snout;
yellow/orange to brown upper body; pale underside; dark patch at the base
of the tail. Underwater lighting can change the apparent colors.
Habitat context: California/Baja kelp forests and rocky reefs.

Use the accompanying images as positive identity examples, not as examples
of every possible pose or life stage. Do not label every elongated yellow
fish as this species. If distinguishing features cannot be seen and identity
is uncertain, mark the entire target image uncertain rather than guess.
